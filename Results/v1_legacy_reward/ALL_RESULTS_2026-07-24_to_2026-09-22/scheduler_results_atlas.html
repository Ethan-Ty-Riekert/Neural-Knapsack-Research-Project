Scheduler Results Atlas

Every evaluation recorded in the project's first phase, for RL policies, classical dispatching heuristics, a metaheuristic (PSO) and an exact solver (CP-SAT). Results are split into offline and online scheduling, and into constant job weights (every wj = 1) and random weights (wj drawn from {1..5}).

Which results these are. This page covers v1 of the project (24 Jul – 22 Sep 2026), when the objective was total (weighted) tardiness ΣwjTj under the original reward. The paper's final results use the revised objective (weighted squared tardiness) and are in Results/v2_objectives/ALL_RESULTS/. Lower tardiness is better.

  1. Headline table (just below): for each of the four settings, the best exact solver, the best heuristic and the best RL policy, and which one wins.
  2. Charts: each chart compares methods on the same instances. In the drop-down, settings marked main are the ones the conclusions rest on; “Other runs” are diagnostics and early experiments.
  3. Key results (the default) shows the best valid result of each method family; All results shows every recorded run. Hover over a bar for its configuration, date and source.
  4. Bars are coloured by method family. Hatched bars are flagged (reached by abandoning jobs, reward hacking or memorisation) or superseded; see Reading notes and the glossary of method names.

The original training plots were never version-controlled; the charts here are regenerated from the recorded numbers (sources in the footer).

Offline

All jobs are known at time 0. The main instance has 100 jobs, 10 machines and horizon 100.

Online

Reading notes

What to keep in mind before quoting any number.

Reward is protocol-local

  • The reward function changed on 08-09 (tardiness ÷ horizon), 09-17 (dense per-tick tardiness, no completion bonus) and 09-20 (hotspot-term fix).
  • Online rewards (~2800) and offline rewards (~280) sit on different scales.
  • Only compare rewards inside one chart.

Weighted vs raw tardiness

  • With constant weights the two are equal, so those panels only show tardiness.
  • Before 18 Sep, weighted runs reported raw tardiness by mistake. Weighted tardiness ΣwjTj is the true objective.

Flagged and superseded

  • Flagged (hatched bars): the number is real, but it was reached by abandoning jobs, reward hacking, memorisation or a collapse. The CP-SAT online bound is also flagged because it sees the future.
  • Superseded: a later, more careful run replaced it, for example after the horizon=110 mistake or a single-seed claim.

    Glossary of methods

    • EDF earliest deadline first; SPT / LPT shortest / longest processing time first; LST least slack time (deadline − duration − now); FCFS first come, first served; WSPT highest weight ÷ duration; ATC apparent tardiness cost (weight, duration and slack combined); Tetris multi-resource packing score.
    • Placement rules: First-Fit (first machine with room), Best-Fit, Worst-Fit, Consolidate (fewest newly active machines).
    • RL Options 1–4: the agent picks a dispatching rule (1), a job by learned priority (2), the same with the ATC index as a feature (3), or job and machine as two choices (4). Original action space: one action per (job, machine) pair.
    • PPO / A2C: the RL training algorithms; RCPO: reward-constrained PPO. CP-SAT: exact constraint-programming solver (Google OR-Tools). PSO: particle swarm optimisation.
